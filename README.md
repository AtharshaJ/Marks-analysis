# School Marks Analysis System

A web-based School Marks Analysis System designed to simplify examination marks management and provide meaningful insights into student academic performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

The School Marks Analysis System aims to digitize the process of managing examination marks and analysing student performance within a school.

The system provides a centralized platform where teachers can enter examination marks, analyse individual and overall academic performance, identify learning gaps, and generate reports.

It is designed to support multiple examination types while providing a simplified and user-friendly workflow for teachers and school administrators.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Simplify examination marks entry and management.
- Reduce manual calculations and paperwork.
- Automate grade calculations and performance analysis.
- Identify students who need additional academic support.
- Provide insights into class-wise, subject-wise, and school-wide performance.
- Maintain historical examination records for performance comparisons.
- Generate professional reports for teachers and school management.

## 📚 Examination Categories

The system is intended to support:

- Grade 5 Scholarship Examination
- G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L)
- G.C.E. Advanced Level (A/L)
- General Information Technology (GIT) Examination
- School Term Tests

Each examination category will support configurable grading criteria and examination-specific analysis.

## 👥 User Roles

The planned system includes the following user roles:

- **Principal:** Access school-wide academic performance and reports.
- **Vice Principal:** Monitor examination results and academic progress.
- **Examination Administrator:** Manage examination configurations and related records.
- **Teachers:** Enter marks for their assigned classes and subjects.
- **Class Teachers:** Access and monitor the academic performance of their respective classes.

Teachers may be assigned to multiple classes and subjects.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Examination Management
- Create and manage examinations.
- Support different examination categories.
- Configure examination-specific grading criteria.
- Maintain historical examination records.

### 2. Student and Academic Management
- Manage student records.
- Organize students by grade and class.
- Manage subject information.
- Associate teachers with multiple classes and subjects.

### 3. Marks Management
- Online marks entry.
- Validate marks before submission.
- Automatically calculate totals, averages, and grades.
- Maintain examination marks records.

**Class-wise Analysis**

- Subject-wise class performance.
- Grade distributions.

**Subject-wise Analysis**
- Subject averages.

- Pass and failure rates.

**School-wide Analysis**
- Overall examination performance.
- Comparison between classes and grades.
- Identification of academic improvement areas.

- Interactive performance dashboards.
- Graphical representations of examination results.

## 🔄 General Workflow

1. An examination administrator creates an examination.
2. Student, class, and subject information is managed.
3. Teachers select their assigned classes and subjects.
4. Teachers enter examination marks online.
5. The system validates marks and calculates grades and statistics.
6. Performance dashboards and analytical reports are generated.
7. Authorized users access, download, or print reports.
